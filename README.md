# Lost in Space (2018)

## 🌌 Overview
**Lost in Space** is a **sci-fi adventure series** that premiered on **Netflix** in *2018*.  
It’s a modern reimagining of the 1965 series of the same name.

---

## 👨‍👩‍👧 The Robinson Family
The story follows the Robinson family as they embark on a mission to colonize a new world but end up stranded on a mysterious planet.

### Main Characters
- **John Robinson** – The father and mission commander  
- **Maureen Robinson** – The brilliant scientist and mother  
- **Will Robinson** – The youngest child and the heart of the story  
- **Judy & Penny Robinson** – Their brave and resourceful daughters  

---
